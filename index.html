BEHIND THE CITY

Manhattan, block by block.

Explore the entire Manhattan borough, from the Battery to Inwood, including its mapped islands. Buildings, street centerlines, shoreline, parks and tree locations come from official NYC datasets.

The world renders with Three.js WebGPU. Buildings stream in as 500 m, 1 km and 2 km tiles; close views add textured facades, architectural setbacks, rooftop equipment, trees and traffic. An orthographic camera, directional shadows, ambient occlusion and crisp edges give it an isometric city-game finish.

Footprints and available roof heights follow the data. Facades, setbacks, crowns, roof props and cars are procedural interpretations. Traffic is simulated, terrain is flat, and park boundaries describe properties rather than exact grass cover. Neighboring NYC boroughs are shown as unmodeled context; the New Jersey shore is outside this dataset.
